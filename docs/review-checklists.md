# External review checklists

Automated validation and browser QA do not replace the reviews below.

## Responsible-AI and data-science review

- Confirm each scenario has enough context for its intended answer.
- Identify choices that could be reasonable under missing assumptions.
- Verify terminology, consequences, and mitigation guidance.
- Check that the game distinguishes decision support from decision authority.
- Confirm uncertainty and escalation are represented accurately.

## HR, privacy, marketing, and copyright review

- Review employment examples for jurisdiction-sensitive claims.
- Review consent, purpose limitation, and least-privilege language.
- Review synthetic endorsement and product-claim examples.
- Review provenance, impersonation, licensing, and derivative-work language.
- Mark educational guidance that must not be interpreted as legal advice.

## Accessibility review

- Complete the game using keyboard only.
- Test focus visibility, fullscreen recovery, and menu operation.
- Review contrast in every departmental palette.
- Test reduced motion, high contrast, and larger text preferences.
- Inspect the HTML companion with screen-reading software.
- Confirm important state is never conveyed by color alone.

## Representative playtest

- Ask the player to explain the objective without assistance.
- Record missed prompts, unclear jumps, and confusing evidence.
- Observe whether questions feel connected to the investigation.
- Ask what each wrong-answer explanation taught.
- Check whether integrity feels informative rather than punitive.
- Record completion time, abandoned floors, and accessibility needs.
