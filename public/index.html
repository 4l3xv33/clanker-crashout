Concept glossary

What the building is teaching

Hallucination

Confidently generated content that is unsupported or false. Plausibility is not evidence.

Grounding

Connecting output to controlled, relevant source material and making that support inspectable.

Human oversight

Meaningful, accountable human judgment—not ceremonial approval after an automated decision.

Least privilege

Granting only the access required for a specific task, for only as long as it is needed.

Provenance

A record of where media or information came from and how it was created or changed.

Contestability

The ability for affected people to understand, challenge, and seek correction of consequential decisions.

Objective failure

When a system successfully optimizes a measure that does not represent the real human goal.
